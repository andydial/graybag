## What the DPDP Act requires you to publish

A Data Fiduciary must publish the **business contact information of a Data Protection Officer
(if applicable) or a person who is able to answer questions about the processing**. In practice,
for a company of our size, that is a named grievance officer reachable without an account.

## Two things worth deciding at the same time

- **Response time you commit to in writing.** The Act expects a response; the policy will state
  a period. Suggested: **within 7 working days, and no later than 30 days**. Anything shorter is
  a promise the support plan (`E17-12`) has to keep.
- **Who covers when that person is away.** A published contact that goes unanswered for a
  fortnight is worse than a generic one, because it is a commitment on record.
