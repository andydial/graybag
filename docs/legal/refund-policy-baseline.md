# Refund Policy

> **Provenance.** This is the lawyer-drafted refund policy as published for the legacy
> GrayBag (Bubble) application, supplied by Andy on 2026-08-11. It is the approved baseline.

At GrayBag, we are committed to delivering a reliable and parent-friendly experience. We
understand the importance of timely and nutritious meals for children, and we strive to
maintain the highest standards of service.

## Order Cancellations by GrayBag

Occasionally, we may need to cancel an order due to unforeseen circumstances, such as:

- Unavailability of selected item(s)
- Delivery constraints (e.g., school closures, access restrictions)
- Technical or operational issues

If GrayBag cancels your order, a full refund will be issued to the original method of payment
within 5–7 business days.

## Refund Eligibility

- Refunds are only processed for orders cancelled by GrayBag.
- In the event of partial fulfillment (e.g., one item unavailable), a proportional refund may
  be issued.
- If an issue arises after delivery, customers must raise a complaint within 24 hours via the
  app or support email.

## General Terms

- Refund timelines may vary based on your bank or payment provider.
- GrayBag reserves the right to refuse refund claims in cases of suspected misuse, fraud, or
  repeated complaints not supported by evidence.
- Refund decisions are made at the sole discretion of GrayBag and are considered final.

## Dispute Resolution

In the unlikely event of a dispute, we encourage you to contact us first. Any disputes arising
out of this policy shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in SAS Nagar
(Mohali), India.

## Contact Us

For questions or concerns, please reach out to us at info@graybag.in or through the in-app
help section.
