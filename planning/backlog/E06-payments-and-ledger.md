## Why this is the riskiest epic

Real money, live users, and the legacy implementation has a public webhook endpoint (`payment_processed`) with no visible server-side signature verification. Stripe is being dropped entirely; Razorpay only.

The de-risking spike now lives in **`E19-01`** and runs in phase 1, long before this epic starts. Do not begin E06 design until it has an answer.

- [x] `E06-07` (risk:critical) (mvp) **Ledger — the one way in.** `0038`. The guarantees already existed (`0001`'s append-only and deferred zero-sum triggers, `0013`'s structural sign convention, `0035`'s accounts); what was missing was a posting API, without which every caller assembles a transaction by hand and the zero-sum trigger is the only thing that notices — at COMMIT, with the statement long gone. `post_ledger_transaction()` refuses fewer than two entries, a non-positive amount (**direction carries the sign and is the only thing that does — a negative credit balances**), an unbalanced posting, an unknown or deactivated account, and a reason code that is not `category = 'ledger'` (the foreign key alone accepts `customer_request`).
